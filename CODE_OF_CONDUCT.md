# Code of conduct

Participation in this project requires respectful, professional collaboration.

Contributors must:

- discuss technical decisions without personal attacks or harassment;
- respect privacy and never post credentials, mailbox content, or personal data;
- accept maintainers' responsibility to protect users and the release process;
- avoid discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behaviour threatens people, users, infrastructure, or the project. Serious or
private conduct reports may be sent to `security@oddie.app`. Reports will be
handled with the minimum disclosure needed to investigate and respond.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads,
community spaces, and private communication about project work.
